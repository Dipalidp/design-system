# Microcopy Guide

Brand voice: warm, encouraging, friendly. Never cold or technical.

## Button Labels

| Component | Generic | Brand version |
|---|---|---|
| Primary CTA | "Submit" | "Let's create!" |
| Book workshop | "Book Now" | "Save my spot 🎨" |
| Newsletter | "Subscribe" | "Send me inspiration" |
| Load more | "Load More" | "Show me more" |
| Delete | "Delete" | "Remove this" |

## Empty States

| Screen | Microcopy |
|---|---|
| No workshops booked | "No workshops yet — your creative journey starts here! 🌸" |
| No saved patterns | "Nothing saved yet — explore our patterns and save your favourites" |
| Search no results | "Hmm, nothing found. Try a different search?" |

## Error Messages

| Error | Message |
|---|---|
| Empty email field | "We need your email to send your confirmation 💌" |
| Invalid email | "That email doesn't look right — can you check it?" |
| Payment failed | "Something went wrong with your payment. Let's try again!" |
| Session full | "This one's full! Join the waitlist and we'll let you know 🎉" |
